# 8-Week Flexibility, Balance & Lean-Body Plan

**Goal:** Noticeably more flexible, better balance, and a leaner body composition in 2 months.

**Structure:** Two alternating 2-week programs — **Program A** (weeks 1–2 and 5–6) and **Program B** (weeks 3–4 and 7–8). When a program repeats, you run it at a higher intensity (see [Progression](#progression) below), so you never plateau and never get bored.

**Time commitment:** 30–50 minutes per day, 6 days per week, 1 full rest day.

**Equipment:** None required. Optional: yoga mat, resistance band, a chair or wall for support.

---

## Weekly Rhythm (both programs)

| Day | Focus | Duration |
|-----|-------|----------|
| Mon | Strength circuit (lean) + short stretch | 40–50 min |
| Tue | Yoga / deep flexibility | 30–40 min |
| Wed | HIIT or incline-walk cardio (lean) + balance drills | 30–40 min |
| Thu | Yoga / deep flexibility | 30–40 min |
| Fri | Strength circuit (lean) + short stretch | 40–50 min |
| Sat | Long low-intensity cardio + balance flow | 40–60 min |
| Sun | Full rest (light walk optional) | — |

**Daily non-negotiable:** a 10-minute morning mobility routine (below), every day including Sunday. Flexibility responds to frequency more than intensity — this is the single highest-leverage habit in the plan.

### Daily 10-Minute Morning Mobility (every day)

1. Cat–cow — 10 slow reps
2. World's greatest stretch — 5 per side
3. Deep bodyweight squat hold — 60 seconds (hold something for support if needed)
4. Standing forward fold — 60 seconds, knees soft
5. Hip flexor lunge stretch — 45 seconds per side
6. Thoracic rotations (kneeling) — 8 per side
7. Single-leg stand, eyes open — 30 seconds per side (progress to eyes closed by week 4)

## Program A — Foundation (Weeks 1–2, repeat harder in Weeks 5–6)

### Mon & Fri — Strength Circuit

3 rounds, 45 seconds work / 15 seconds rest per exercise, 90 seconds between rounds:

1. Bodyweight squats
2. Push-ups (knees or incline if needed)
3. Reverse lunges (alternating) — *also trains balance*
4. Glute bridges
5. Plank
6. Bird-dog (slow, controlled) — *balance + core*

Finish with 8 minutes of static stretching: hamstrings, quads, chest, hips — 45 seconds per stretch, exhale into each one.

### Tue & Thu — Flexibility / Yoga (30–40 min)

Flow through, holding each pose 45–60 seconds per side unless noted:

- Sun salutations × 5 rounds (warm-up)
- Downward dog → runner's lunge → half splits (each side)
- Lizard pose (each side)
- Pigeon pose (each side)
- Seated forward fold
- Butterfly stretch
- Supine spinal twist (each side)
- Finish: tree pose, 45 seconds per side — *balance*

### Wed — Cardio + Balance (30–35 min)

- 20 min steady cardio: brisk incline walk, cycling, or easy jog (conversational pace)
- Balance block (10 min):
  - Single-leg stand — 3 × 30 sec per side
  - Heel-to-toe walk (tightrope walk) — 3 × 20 steps
  - Single-leg reach (hinge at hip, reach forward) — 3 × 8 per side
  - Standing knee raise + hold 5 sec at top — 2 × 6 per side

### Sat — Long Cardio + Balance Flow (40–60 min)

- 30–45 min zone-2 cardio (walk, hike, bike, swim — you should be able to talk)
- 10 min slow yoga balance flow: tree → warrior III → half moon (use a wall), 30 seconds per pose per side

## Program B — Progression (Weeks 3–4, repeat harder in Weeks 7–8)

### Mon & Fri — Strength Circuit (unilateral emphasis)

3 rounds, 40 seconds work / 20 seconds rest:

1. Bulgarian split squats (rear foot on chair) — *big balance stimulus*
2. Pike push-ups or standard push-ups
3. Single-leg glute bridge
4. Lateral lunges (alternating)
5. Side plank — 30 sec per side
6. Dead bug (slow)

Finish with 8 minutes of static stretching as in Program A.

### Tue & Thu — Deeper Flexibility (35–40 min)

Same structure as Program A, but holds increase to 90 seconds and add:

- Low squat (malasana) hold — 2 × 60 sec
- Frog pose — 2 × 60 sec
- Standing wide-leg forward fold
- Couch stretch (rear foot up against wall) — 60 sec per side
- Finish: eagle pose or dancer's pose — 30 sec per side — *harder balance*

### Wed — HIIT + Balance (30 min)

- Warm up 5 min
- HIIT: 30 sec hard / 90 sec easy × 8 rounds (bike sprints, hill runs, fast stair climbs, or burpees)
- Balance block (10 min):
  - Single-leg stand **eyes closed** — 3 × 20 sec per side
  - Single-leg stand on a pillow/cushion — 3 × 30 sec per side
  - Skater hops (stick the landing, hold 2 sec) — 3 × 8 per side
  - Clock reaches (stand on one leg, tap toe to 12/3/6/9 o'clock) — 2 rounds per side

### Sat — Long Cardio + Balance Flow (45–60 min)

- 40–50 min zone-2 cardio
- 10 min balance flow: warrior III (no wall) → half moon → standing figure-4 squat, 30–45 seconds per pose per side

## Progression

When a program repeats (A in weeks 5–6, B in weeks 7–8), make it harder:

- **Strength:** add a 4th round, or slow every rep to a 3-second lowering phase
- **Flexibility:** add 15–30 seconds to each hold; sink slightly deeper (stretch should be intense but never sharp pain)
- **Balance:** close your eyes, stand on a cushion, or add slow head turns while balancing
- **Cardio/HIIT:** add 2 HIIT rounds, or 10 minutes to zone-2 sessions

## Getting Leaner: The Nutrition Side

Training alone rarely produces visible leanness — a modest calorie deficit does. Keep it simple:

- **Small deficit:** eat roughly 300–500 kcal below maintenance. Aim to lose ~0.5% of body weight per week — slow enough to keep muscle.
- **Protein first:** ~1.6–2.2 g per kg of body weight daily. It preserves muscle and keeps you full.
- **Mostly whole foods:** vegetables, fruit, lean protein, legumes, whole grains. Liquid calories (soda, juice, alcohol) are the easiest thing to cut.
- **Sleep 7–9 hours:** poor sleep measurably increases hunger and reduces fat loss.
- **Don't chase the scale daily:** weigh weekly at the same time, or track by how clothes fit and progress photos every 2 weeks.

## Tracking Progress

Test on day 1, end of week 4, and end of week 8:

| Test | Measures |
|------|----------|
| Sit-and-reach (fingers toward toes, legs straight) | Hamstring/back flexibility |
| Single-leg stand, eyes closed (seconds until wobble) | Balance |
| Deep squat hold comfort (seconds) | Hip/ankle mobility |
| Waist measurement + progress photo | Leanness |
| Max push-ups in one set | Strength retention while leaning out |

## Rules of the Road

1. **Never stretch cold.** Always after the warm-up or workout, or after the morning mobility gets blood flowing.
2. **Stretch to intensity, not pain.** A strong pull is good; sharp or nervy pain means back off immediately.
3. **Consistency beats heroics.** Six moderate days beat two brutal ones. If life happens, do the 10-minute morning mobility and call it a win.
4. **Missed a day?** Skip it and continue — never double up.
5. If you have injuries, joint conditions, or are new to exercise, check with a physician or physical therapist before starting.
