About Donna

A scientist’s eye,
an artist’s hand

Donna knows exactly what a PhD defense feels like — she did her own doctoral research at Amsterdam UMC on work participation for people with chronic illness. Her live-painting career began almost by accident: asked to illustrate a scientific conference, she drew the keynote speakers and their stories on the spot, and the response was overwhelming.

One request led to another, and painting defense ceremonies became her specialty — she has since captured roughly two hundred promotions live from the audience. Weddings followed naturally: the same craft of watching closely and distilling a once-in-a-lifetime moment onto paper, in real time.

Today Donna works as a full-time artist and is one of the Netherlands’ go-to live painters for events.

Get in touch

Send the date, venue and occasion. Dates fill up quickly, so reach out early — surprise gifts for a PhD candidate are very welcome.

She blends in

Donna works quietly from the audience or the side of the room. Fineliner and watercolor on paper — no easels in the way, no wet-paint worries for the venue.

Painted live

The scene takes shape in real time: the couple saying yes, or the committee firing questions. Years of practice, down to a refined and fast routine.

Handed over

Finished, packaged and tied with a bow on the spot — often before the reception line forms. One unique artwork of one unrepeatable day.
